FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Are you licensed for plumbing work in Ayr?

Yes, licensed and insured across Waterloo Region. Permitted jobs in Ayr are filed with the Region and inspected, which matters at sale time because buyers here check the paperwork closely.

Is emergency service available in Ayr and area?

Day and night. Sump failures during a Nith high-water event and burst pipes in a January cold snap are exactly the calls we keep the phone answered for.

How soon can a failed water heater be swapped?

Usually same or next day. Subdivision homes around Ayr share a handful of common setups, so we arrive with the likely replacement on the truck and confirm the sizing on site.

Our 1970s house has poly-B pipe. Should we be worried?

Reasonably, yes. Polybutylene fails at fittings without warning, and the insurance questions around it keep growing. We can replace the exposed runs affordably now and plan the in-wall sections, or quote the full repipe if the whole system is original.

Is Region of Waterloo water really that hard?

It is, because most of it is pumped groundwater. A properly sized softener protects the water heater, the dishwasher, and every faucet in the house, and it earns its cost back in appliance life.

Can you plumb a legal second suite?

Yes. Separate shut-offs, proper backflow protection, and permitted rough-ins for basement apartments are common work around Ayr given the demand from KW renters.

What does your quoting look like?

Written quotes before work begins, with parts and labour broken out clearly. If a century home hides a surprise behind a wall, we pause, show you, and re-quote before going further.

Which communities do you serve around Ayr?

Ayr, St. George, Branchton, and Roseville directly, with regular work into New Hamburg, Baden, Paris, and south Kitchener. Rural properties between the Grand and the Nith are all in range.
